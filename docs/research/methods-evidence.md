# Methodology: evidence and limits

This is a map from implemented design choices to relevant research, not a claim that prior peer review validated this implementation, its outcomes, or its deployment safety. The code describes what was run; the frozen protocol and manifests establish the version and execution details. See [the study protocol](../protocols/task-provenance-v1.md), [related work](related-work.md), and [the report](../../report/draft.md). The review below is limited to the sources named; it is not an audit of every bibliography entry.

**Verification scope (7 November 2026):** primary conference/workshop records and abstracts were checked for ConfAIde, REALM and AxBench; journal records were checked for Wilson and McNemar, the full text for Fagerland, publisher-deposited metadata/abstract for Efron, and indexed Oxford metadata for Clopper–Pearson. Chen's extraction section and Nief's author-warning metadata were also checked. Existing preprint/source notes remain useful, but an arXiv record alone does not establish an accepted venue. This is not an audit of every bibliography entry or a replication of the cited experiments.

| Treat messages as untrusted when prompting the monitor. | [provenance.py](../../experiments/composition-gap/provenance.py) `MONITOR` marks proposed messages untrusted; the gate fails closed on malformed verdicts or errors, with failed trials excluded. | Lee and Tiwari, *Prompt Infection* ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07283), `lee2024prompt`): recorded as a preprint; peer-reviewed venue not verified. | This is a prompt-level instruction, not a verified isolation boundary. The study does not inject adversarial instructions into proposed messages or measure injection resistance; no such robustness claim follows. |
| Use common tasks for matched comparisons and shuffle execution order. | [harness.py](../../experiments/composition-gap/harness.py) `make_task(seed)` deterministically samples vocabulary and positions; [provenance.py](../../experiments/composition-gap/provenance.py) builds crossed jobs and shuffles with a fixed scheduling seed. | No external experimental-design source independently verified for this audit. | This is randomized task generation and scheduling, not randomized assignment of each task to only one condition. Seeds label matched tasks, not proof of independent sampling from a deployment population. Concurrent execution means a shuffled submission order need not be completion order. |
| Quantify uncertainty and compare paired binary outcomes. | [analyze.py](../../experiments/composition-gap/analyze.py) `wilson`, `boot_ci`, `mcnemar_p`, and paired contrasts. | McNemar (1947), *Note on the Sampling Error of the Difference Between Correlated Proportions or Percentages* ([publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02295996), `mcnemar1947sampling`), and Fagerland, Lydersen and Laake (2013), *The McNemar test for binary matched-pairs data: mid-p and asymptotic are better than exact conditional* ([journal article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1471-2288-13-91), `fagerland2013mcnemar`): primary journal records fetched by a team member on 2026-11-07. Fagerland discusses the exact conditional test and its conservatism. The original McNemar article is historical; the implemented exact conditional variant is described explicitly by Fagerland et al. Do not silently switch to their recommended mid-p variant after seeing results. | Intervals and p-values require appropriate sampling and pair assumptions; common seed does not ensure independence across seeds. Small or all-zero samples, missing or failed trials, multiple comparisons, model drift and post-pilot choices limit inference. A bootstrap interval can degenerate with few discordances. Significance does not prove causation beyond the study design or external generality. |

The held-out study's prespecified comparison and exclusions should be read directly in the protocol and frozen run documentation. The use of seeded generation, a shuffled queue, or a commit alone does not establish preregistration, blinding, independence, representativeness, or an effect in real systems. Peer-reviewed literature can justify why a question is worth testing; it cannot certify this experiment's implementation or empirical results.

## Additional statistical foundations

| Method in code | Checked source and status | Implementation and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| `analyze.wilson` | Wilson (1927), *Probable Inference, the Law of Succession, and Statistical Inference*, JASA 22(158):209–212 ([original journal record](https://www.jstor.org/stable/2276774), `wilson1927probable`). | Two-sided 95% score interval. Formula checked against the code; publication of the original method does not establish sampling independence here. |
| `analyze.boot_ci` | Efron (1979), *Bootstrap Methods: Another Look at the Jackknife*, Annals of Statistics 7(1):1–26 ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176344552), `efron1979bootstrap`). Publisher-deposited metadata and abstract checked via [Crossref](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1214/aos/1176344552). | Foundational bootstrap reference, not certification of this cluster design. The code resamples whole task-seed clusters with replacement and uses 2,000 percentile replicates with a fixed analysis RNG. Repeated generations remain in their task cluster. |
| Zero-event discussion / `check_claims.c_zero_bound` | Clopper and Pearson (1934), *The Use of Confidence or Fiducial Limits Illustrated in the Case of the Binomial*, Biometrika 26(4):404–413 ([indexed publisher record](https://academic.oup.com/biomet/article-abstract/26/4/404/291538), `clopper1934confidence`). Metadata checked; full text not retrieved. | Under an iid Bernoulli model, zero events in fifty trials gives the one-sided 95% upper bound `1 - 0.05**(1/50)`, about 5.8%. It is not the two-sided Wilson interval or an external-validity guarantee. |

A percentile-bootstrap interval and an exact conditional test are different procedures. Five favorable discordant pairs and none unfavorable yield exact two-sided McNemar `p = 0.0625`, even when a percentile-bootstrap interval excludes zero. Interpret the frozen exact test as specified, preserve the interval as descriptive, and report all paired counts and multiplicity. Do not select a method because it produces a more favorable result.

The SAE proposal has no empirical result in this repository. Published precedents motivate controls, not a claim that the proposed intervention already works. Similarly, the source map does not upgrade Jones, Prompt Infection, the sequential-monitor preprint, Shen's [AI Organizations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10290) (`shen2026organizations`), incident reports, or software cards to peer-reviewed status without a verified venue. Unknown review status is not proof that a paper was never reviewed.
