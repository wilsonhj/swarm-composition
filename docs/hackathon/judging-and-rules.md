# Judging criteria and rules

Sources: AIAF page https://aialignmentfoundation.org/hackathons/swarm-alignment-2026 and Apart page https://apartresearch.com/sprints/swarm-alignment-2026 (both fetched 2026-11-06). The two pages give DIFFERENT criteria for the two rounds, so both are listed.

## Judging criteria - LA hub round

Headings verbatim (AIAF page). Judges assess both the 3-minute video and the live presentation under these four:

1. **Idea: novelty & relevance** - is the idea new and does it fit the topic.
2. **Soundness & execution** - is the work technically careful and well done.
3. **Results** - what the project actually found or demonstrated.
4. **Clarity & presentation** - how clearly it is communicated.

Live presentations have a larger judging panel; in-person audience votes count as one extra judge. Source: AIAF page.

## Judging criteria - Global sprint

Headings verbatim (Apart page). Each project is scored by expert judges on three dimensions, each 1 to 5:

1. **"Dimension 1: Impact Potential & Innovation"** - how much it would matter if it succeeded and how novel it is; a 4 or 5 needs something new to the field, not a replication.
2. **"Dimension 2: Execution Quality"** - soundness of methodology, implementation and findings.
3. **"Dimension 3: Presentation & Clarity"** - how clearly problem, approach, results and impact are communicated.

Individual judge scores are kept internal; judges give constructive feedback without reviewer names. Source: Apart page.

Implication: replication-style work scores low on Dimension 1; novelty matters in both rounds.

## Integrity rules (Apart page)

- Weekend-only work: only work done during the weekend is judged. Prior work must be clearly stated. Every submission is screened for this.
- Pre-existing code: must be marked as pre-existing in the README and in the report.
- Citations and results: made-up results or references can disqualify a project (the page says "made-up results or references"; it does not use the word "fabricated"). The team is responsible for all content.
- AI tools: allowed for reasoning, debugging, code and writing, but the team is responsible for all content.
- Dual-use / ethics: the report must include the appendix "Limitations and Dual-Use / Ethical Considerations" (mandatory). Projects are hidden from the website until after judging because of infohazard concerns.
- Clarity: judges may decline to score a report that is too unclear.
- Unfinished work may be submitted; honest limitations are welcome.

The AIAF page lists no integrity or conduct rules.

## Eligibility and teams

- Free to enter (AIAF page).
- Anyone from any country; no prior AI safety experience or specific background required (Apart page). The AIAF page states no age, residency or other eligibility limits.
- Solo or teams. AIAF page: up to five; solo entrants can team up on the day. Apart page: up to 5 recommended, larger teams allowed.
- Each person may be on at most two projects (Apart page).
- Team forming happens on Discord (Apart page).
- The same project may be submitted to both the LA and global rounds (AIAF page).
- Prize terms govern awards: https://apartresearch.com/sprints/prize-terms (linked on the Apart page; not fetched, so terms are unread).
